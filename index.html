Investor & Agency Guidance

Official development details regarding the Kogi Invest One-Stop Centre (KIOSC) under KOSIPPPPA.

The development plan establishes a 2 months + 2 weeks (10-week) delivery schedule across 7 structured milestones, commencing immediately upon the release of funds. During this period, all investment inquiries, filings, and stakeholder requests are actively received and handled by KOSIPPPPA.
Investors and enterprise representatives can contact KOSIPPPPA directly via email at info@kosippppa.kogistate.gov.ng, by calling +234 803 615 7486 or +234 803 655 4498 during working hours (Monday – Thursday, 8:00 am – 4:00 pm), or by submitting the online inquiry form above.
KIOSC delivers 6 integrated components (Slide 4): (1) Public Website & CMS for opportunities and sectors, (2) Investor Portal with registration and multi-step application wizard, (3) Agency Workspace for KOSIPPPPA case triage and routing, (4) Scoped MDA Desks for technical reviews, (5) Payment Operations with fee catalogue and reconciliation, and (6) Dynamic Admin & Superadmin Engine for forms, workflows, and audit reporting.
The GRM (Slide 13) is an independent, traceable 5-step ticket workflow (File → Acknowledge → Route → Investigate → Resolve) designed to resolve investor delays, disputes, fee inquiries, or technical issues with full audit trail and SLA tracking.
Security controls (Slide 5, 17) include PostgreSQL database Row Level Security (RLS), Supabase authentication with role-based access control, optional/mandated MFA for privileged accounts, encrypted private file storage with short-lived signed links and malware scanning, and immutable audit logging.
